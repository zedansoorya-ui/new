# Suggested clips

These are prompts, not scripts. Say each idea in your own words, the way you would actually
dictate it, and write down what you really said as the `reference`. Aim for at least five clips
per group; the Hinglish group can be smaller.

## Casual (messages)

- Ask a friend whether they are free this evening and suggest a place.
- Tell someone you are running ten minutes late and why.
- Reply to a group chat about weekend plans, with a joke.
- Say thanks for a gift and what you liked about it.
- Ask a classmate which pages the homework covers.

## Fast

- Read a paragraph of any article quickly, without pausing.
- Give directions to your house at speed.
- Rattle off a shopping list of eight items.
- Summarise yesterday in one breath.
- Explain a rule of a sport quickly.

## Self-corrections

- "Let's meet at three — no, wait, four."
- Change a recipient's name mid-sentence ("send it to Arjun, sorry, to Rohan").
- Restart a sentence halfway ("I think we should — actually, let's not.").
- Correct a number ("it costs two hundred, I mean two hundred and fifty").
- Use "scratch that" to drop the previous sentence.
- Correct a day ("on Tuesday, no, Thursday").

## Lists

- Three things to pack for a trip.
- Steps to set up a new laptop, said as "first… then… finally…".
- Agenda items for a meeting.
- Pros and cons of two options.
- A to-do list with numbers ("one, email the teacher; two…").

## Names and terms from your dictionary

- Dictate a sentence with your own name and two friends' names.
- Mention your school, city and a teacher.
- Use three technical or subject terms you use often (IB, TOK, an MUN committee name…).
- Spell out an email address.
- Mention an app or brand name with unusual spelling.

## Hinglish (a few, to document Parakeet's limits)

- A casual message that mixes Hindi and English ("kal ka plan kya hai, let me know").
- Directions mixing both languages.
- A sentence with an English technical term inside Hindi.
- A quick reply that switches language mid-sentence ("haan, I'll be there by 5").

## Code (for Terminal, Xcode, VS Code)

- "git commit dash m fix the login bug".
- A Swift function signature said aloud.
- A file path ("slash users slash zedan slash documents").
- A variable name in camelCase.
- A terminal command with flags.
- A short SQL query.
